# ABE SEO Tool Stack (decided 2026-11-05)

Key insight: ABE has 100+ locations, so local search (Google Business Profile)
drives more results than the website's own rankings.

## Free stack (starting point)
- Website: Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools (import from GSC),
  Ahrefs Webmaster Tools, GA4 (Consumer property exists), Looker Studio dashboards
- Site launch: Screaming Frog free (500 URLs), PageSpeed Insights, Rich Results Test,
  Schema.org Validator
- Local: Google Business Profile Manager (bulk spreadsheet edits), Apple Business
  Connect, Bing Places, Yelp for Business
- Keywords/AI: Google Keyword Planner, Google Trends, Otterly.AI free tier or a monthly
  manual check of 10-15 ChatGPT/Gemini questions ("best brunch in Orlando")

## Paid, if needed
- Listings + reviews (biggest gap in the free tools): Yext, SOCi, Birdeye, Uberall,
  Rio SEO, Malou. Check with ops/franchise marketing first; they may already pay for one.
- SEO suite: Semrush (well-rounded) or Ahrefs (backlinks)

## Website relaunch checklist
- Crawl the old site now and save every URL
- Contractors map 301 redirects (location and menu pages especially)
- Crawl staging and the live site after launch: broken links, missing titles, stray noindex
- Verify Restaurant/LocalBusiness + Menu schema on location pages
- Verify GSC on the new site before launch; submit the sitemap
